Started as a Research Associate in Advanced Materials at King's College London

Xuezhou Wang joined the Department of Engineering, Faculty of Natural, Mathematical & Engineering Sciences, King's College London, as a Research Associate in Advanced Materials, based at the Strand Campus.

As a computational materials scientist, his work focuses on multiscale modelling and simulation of microstructure evolution, defect kinetics, and phase transformation in metallic alloys, with particular emphasis on multicomponent aluminium alloys. His approach bridges first-principles calculations, atomistic simulations, and mesoscale numerical models to understand how composition and processing conditions affect microstructure and material performance for structural applications.

At King's, he extends this multiscale modelling framework to titanium, nickel, and high-entropy alloys, while integrating machine learning and AI-driven methods for advanced materials design. His current research interests span microstructure evolution modelling, defect thermodynamics and diffusion kinetics, phase stability, and AI for materials.
