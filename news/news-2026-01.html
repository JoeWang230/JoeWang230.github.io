Paper published in Materials Today

Our collaborative paper has been published in Materials Today (Vol. 92, pp. 215–225, 2026):

Shenghua Wu†, Bin Chen†, Xuezhou Wang†, Fuzhi Liu, Hang Xue, Chong Yang, Peng Zhang, Jinyu Zhang, Yuqing Wang, Shengwu Guo, Yong Peng, Gang Liu, Yanjun Li, Alexis Deschamps, Jijun Yang, Guang Ran, Anmin Nie, and Jun Sun. "Unprecedently radiation-tolerant Al alloys via vacancy-mediated anomalous solid-state phase transformation." Materials Today 92 (2026) 215–225.

This work reports an anomalous solid-state phase transformation pathway in aluminium alloys mediated by excess vacancies introduced during irradiation. The resulting microstructure confers unprecedented radiation tolerance, with implications for structural materials in energy and aerospace applications.
